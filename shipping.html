Shipping

A complete policy page for the prototype, written as demo content rather than a real fulfillment promise.

Demo delivery options

Standard delivery is modeled as 4–7 business days after dispatch. Orders above $150 receive free standard shipping in this prototype; smaller orders use an $18 demo rate.

Tracking

A production implementation would create shipment events from the commerce or fulfillment platform and surface a carrier tracking link here and in order history.

Large pieces

Furniture can require different rates, delivery windows, and appointment scheduling. This demo keeps the logic intentionally simple, but the interface has room for those rules.

Prototype note

No actual shipping service, carrier, warehouse, or geographic coverage is claimed by Stillward.
